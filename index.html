Case Study

APPROACH & RESULTS

Approach

  • Mix method research for both exploratory and validatory information
  • Inclusion of diverse profiles of gamers from casual to hardcore, regular to occasional in family or social events gamers across all age groups
  • A/B Concept Testing method wherein each respondent shown different console concepts, pricing, and feature sets, and their preferences were measured.

Solutions & Support
